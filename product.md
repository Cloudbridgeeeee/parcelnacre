# Product

ParcelNacre: Resolve delivery exceptions intelligently.

AI parcel exception management software that classifies failed delivery reports and identifies recurring causes of unsuccessful fulfilment.

- Founder and CEO: Olawale Yusuf
- Co-founder and CTO: Prisca Ezeani
- Industry: Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Ecommerce
- Customers: Courier companies, ecommerce retailers and fulfilment providers
- Core features: Exception classification, delivery issue analytics, resolution recommendations, shipment history, performance reporting
- MVP: Delivery report imports and exception dashboards
- Planned AWS: Bedrock, SageMaker AI, S3, DynamoDB, Lambda
- Business model: Shipment-volume subscriptions
- Main action: Book a demo
