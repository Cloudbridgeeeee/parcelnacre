How ParcelNacre onboarding runs

  1. Delivery report imports

    Send delivery reports and scan events as CSV or through your courier platform’s export.

  2. Exception dashboards by zone

    Within a week each zone shows its failure rate and the causes that recur most.

  3. Weekly cause and fix reviews

    Each week operations leads review suggested fixes and log a decision on each.

Ask about a demo

What data do you need?

Delivery reports and scan events: parcel ID, zone, rider, attempt time, status and rider note.

Does it change our routes?

No. It reads imports only. Your team decides every change.

What does it cost?

A subscription priced by monthly shipment volume. We quote after a demo.

Do you store customer names?

No. Names and phone numbers are removed on import; only the zone is kept.

Where is our data kept?

In an encrypted AWS store for your company alone, deleted on request.
